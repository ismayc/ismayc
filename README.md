## Selected work

- 📖 **[ModernDive](https://www.moderndive.com/v2)** — *Statistical Inference via Data Science*, a free online textbook (with a [print edition](https://www.routledge.com/Statistical-Inference-via-Data-Science-A-ModernDive-into-R-and-the-Tidyverse/Ismay-Kim-Valdivia/p/book/9781032708379)) teaching statistics through computation, R, and the tidyverse — used in courses around the world.

- 🎓 **[thesisdown](https://github.com/ismayc/thesisdown)** — thesis templates in R Markdown, adapted by dozens of universities.

## Recent analyses

Evidence-based basketball research across the NBA, WNBA, and G League — collected at **[Basketball Data Science](https://ismayc.github.io/basketball-data-science/)**. Some highlights:

- 🏀 **[The Mebound](https://ismayc.github.io/mebounds/)** — how often do players rebound their own misses? A WNBA/NBA study of Angel Reese's recovery rates.
- 📏 **[Are NBA players really getting shorter?](https://ismayc.github.io/jersey-height-study/)** — 45 seasons of roster data put the height-decline narrative to the test.
- 🎯 **[Shot quality across three leagues](https://ismayc.github.io/shot-quality-study/)** — an xMake/xPTS model spanning the NBA, WNBA, and G League.
- 🧮 **[Draft value by team and college](https://ismayc.github.io/draft-potential-by-team/)** — pricing draft picks across 27 classes, with an interactive viewer.
- ⏱️ **[The end-of-quarter 2-for-1](https://ismayc.github.io/playbyplay-study/)** — does the late-clock strategy actually pay off? A full season of play-by-play says…
- 📈 **[Player and lineup value with honest error bars](https://ismayc.github.io/lineup-valuation-study/)** — RAPM-family valuation that owns its uncertainty.
- 🛰️ **[Ten years of NBA tracking data](https://ismayc.github.io/tracking-study/)** — movement trends from 7.5 million SportVU frames.
