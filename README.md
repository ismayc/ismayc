## Recent analyses

Evidence-based basketball research across the NBA, WNBA, and G League, collected at **[Basketball Data Science](https://chesterismay.com/basketball-data-science/)**. Some highlights:

- 🏀 **[The Mebound](https://chesterismay.com/mebounds/)** — how often do players rebound their own misses? A WNBA/NBA study of Angel Reese's recovery rates.
- 📏 **[Are NBA players really getting shorter?](https://chesterismay.com/jersey-height-study/)** — 45 seasons of roster data put the height-decline narrative to the test.
- 🎯 **[Shot quality across three leagues](https://chesterismay.com/shot-quality-study/)** — an xMake/xPTS model spanning the NBA, WNBA, and G League.
- 🧮 **[Draft value by team and college](https://chesterismay.com/draft-potential-by-team/)** — pricing draft picks across 27 classes, with an interactive viewer.
- ⏱️ **[The end-of-quarter 2-for-1](https://chesterismay.com/playbyplay-study/)** — does the late-clock strategy actually pay off? A full season of play-by-play says…
- 📈 **[Player and lineup value with honest error bars](https://chesterismay.com/lineup-valuation-study/)** — RAPM-family valuation that owns its uncertainty.
- 🛰️ **[Ten years of NBA tracking data](https://chesterismay.com/tracking-study/)** — movement trends from 7.5 million SportVU frames.
